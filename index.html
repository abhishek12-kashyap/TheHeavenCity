01Is Heaven City RERA-approved?+
Yes. The Heaven City Phase III is registered with the Uttar Pradesh Real Estate Regulatory Authority under registration number UPRERAPRJ0987432. The certificate is available in our Legal Documents section above and will be shared in original at the time of your site visit.
02What is the payment schedule?+
10% at booking (refundable within 15 days), 20% at agreement, 40% linked to infrastructure milestones (roads, electricity, water), 30% at possession and registry. All milestones are verified by third-party quality auditors.
03Are home loans available?+
Yes — the project is pre-approved by HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak Mahindra, and Punjab National Bank. Loans are typically sanctioned within 8–12 working days. Our finance advisors can process paperwork on your behalf at no charge.
04What is included in the plot price?+
The advertised price is fully inclusive of: BSP, EDC/IDC, PLC for corner and park-facing plots, road development, electricity and water infrastructure, boundary wall, and one-time maintenance corpus for the first two years. GST and registry are additional as per Government of India rates.
05Can I resell my plot before possession?+
Yes. After 20% payment, plots may be transferred to a third party at a nominal ₹25,000 transfer fee. There is no lock-in on resale, and Yatharth maintains a resale marketplace for owners who wish to exit their position.
06When is possession?+
Registry-ready possession is scheduled for Q2 2027. Phase III is our fastest-tracked project — 80% of infrastructure is already completed as of this quarter. Delays, if any, are covered by penalty clauses in the buyer's agreement.
07How do I book a site visit?+
Use the form below, WhatsApp us at +91 98765 43210, or call directly. Site visits are complimentary — we arrange pickup and drop from Delhi/NCR, and a plot advisor walks you through the entire township, master plan, and legal documents.
08What about NRI buyers?+
NRIs can purchase under FEMA guidelines using NRE/NRO accounts. We assist with power of attorney documentation, virtual site tours, and complete paperwork remotely. Our NRI desk operates in three time zones.
